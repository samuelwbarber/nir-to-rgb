PSNR rewards pixels, not meaning

Three images compared to the same reference. PSNR is computed live from the actual pixels (higher = "closer" to PSNR).

A red postbox
A completely different thing. No person at all — but it keeps the big red region.
…
 
PSNR scores the postbox as the better match, even though it is not a person, while it scores the same person in a different shirt as a poor match. A per-pixel metric cannot see meaning: it just counts how many pixels happen to line up. This is why this project judges a translation by how much frozen-model behaviour it recovers (gap closure), not by PSNR.

Numbers are real: PSNR = 10·log10(255²/MSE), computed in your browser from the pixels above.
