SSIM scores the red "stop" as almost identical to green, because it ignores colour and only sees the bright disk in the same place. Yet it marks a harmless darker exposure of the correct green light as a worse match. For NIR→RGB, colour often is the meaning (signals, vegetation, materials), so a structural metric cannot certify it. This is why the project measures downstream behaviour (gap closure), not SSIM.

Real SSIM: Gaussian 11×11 window, C1=(0.01·255)², C2=(0.03·255)², computed on luminance in your browser.
